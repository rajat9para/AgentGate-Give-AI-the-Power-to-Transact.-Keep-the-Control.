# 🎤 RazorX — Project Pitch

**The industry is rapidly shifting from conversational AI assistants into autonomous agents that can plan and research, but the moment an AI tries to actually buy something, the entire workflow breaks.** Because Generative AI models are probabilistic, they hallucinate numbers, miscalculate pricing tiers, and are inherently vulnerable to prompt injections. Giving an LLM direct access to raw credit cards or unconstrained payment APIs is an unacceptable financial risk. As a result, today’s shopping assistants are forced to stop right before checkout and dump a manual payment link back to a human, completely destroying the promise of true agentic automation.

**RazorX (AgentGate) solves this fundamental bottleneck by building a Zero-Trust Cryptographic Execution Gateway between autonomous AI agents and payment rails.** Instead of micro-approving every single purchase, the user sets their spending rules once—such as single-transaction ceilings, daily/weekly velocity limits, and whitelisted categories. The AI operates strictly in the reasoning and negotiation plane, discovering products across merchant catalogs and bargaining for discounts. Once an agreement is formed, our **Deterministic Mathematical Policy Engine validates every financial constraint and issues an Ed25519 asymmetric digital signature (RFC 8032)** that cryptographically binds the exact amount, merchant ID, user ID, and a one-time nonce. If a transient banking issue occurs, an autonomous recovery agent automatically switches to pre-authorized fallback rails (like Card) in under two seconds.

**Ultimately, RazorX unlocks the trillion-dollar agentic commerce economy by making AI transacting safe, autonomous, and mathematically provable.** Consumers enjoy frictionless purchasing with **100% mathematical protection against overspending**, merchants capture high-intent autonomous AI buyers while protecting gross profit margins, and payment networks process high-velocity agentic transactions with full trust. Every transaction is backed by an **Explainable Decision Card, a GST-compliant tax invoice, and a tamper-evident SHA-256 Merkle audit chain**, proving that you don't have to choose between AI autonomy and financial control—you can have both.
